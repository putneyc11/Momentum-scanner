# HYP-008 — pre-register the screen experiment against data that does not exist yet

**Status:** building
**Date:** 2026-08-29  **Pod:** (discovery — `lib/data.js` recording, not a live threshold change)
**Instrument:** not yet built. No result may be read before the day counts below. The 144-day library is not this experiment and must not be used as a negative class.

## Claim

After **40** complete sessions of admitted-plus-stratified-reject tapes, names the live screen admitted, entered at the first poll they appeared in the returned universe (P0), have a +5m wall-clock mean at least **50 bps above the sham class (P5)** and at least **20 bps above the pooled near-miss class (N1)**; failing either, the current screen has no measured value.

## Category

(a) discovery recording in `lib/data.js` (and the EOD record path in `engine.js`). **No live floor, rank cutoff, or traded universe changes in this HYP.** Threshold verdicts below are pre-registered questions for a later (a) HYP, scored only after the 40-day unblinding.

## Mechanism

Every recorded day file is downstream of `discover()`: price and pct filters, then rank-slice, then `noteMovers` on that already-filtered list (`lib/data.js:209-210`). The names the screen threw away were never fetched, so the layer Fizz's trigger decomposition pointed at cannot be compared to a negative class, and cannot be recovered for any past day. A census of failing values is cheap (snapshots are already in memory every 90s); 1-minute tape is expensive, so only a predeclared stratified sample of near-misses and a sham class get tape — far rejects are not a control, they are a different market.

## Falsifier

Written before any negative-class day exists. The screen has no value, on the 40-day unblinding, if **either**:

1. **P0 +5m mean ≤ P5 +5m mean + 50 bps**, or
2. **P0 +5m mean ≤ N1 (pooled near-miss) +5m mean + 20 bps**.

Specific thresholds, also written now, are wrong rather than merely arbitrary if:

3. **`MAX_UNIVERSE 20` too tight:** causal ranks 21–40, entered at first poll, +5m mean ≥ ranks 1–20 +5m mean (not worse). We are discarding equals.
4. **`MAX_UNIVERSE 20` too loose:** ranks 11–20 +5m mean ≤ P5 +5m mean, while ranks 1–10 beat P5 by the 50 bps margin. The extra ten are noise.
5. **`RTH_PCT_FLOOR 25` arbitrary:** the RTH near-miss band 15% ≤ pct < 25% (N1-RTH), first-cross of 15%, +5m mean is within 20 bps of the admitted 25–35% band's P0 +5m mean.
6. **`RTH_PCT_FLOOR 25` too low:** admitted RTH 25–35% P0 +5m ≤ P5, while admitted ≥35% beats P5 by 50 bps.
7. **`PM_PCT_FLOOR 10` arbitrary:** PM near-miss 5% ≤ pct < 10%, first-cross of 5%, +5m mean within 20 bps of admitted PM 10–15% P0.
8. **`MIN_PRICE` should be $2:** admitted names with price-at-first-poll < $2 have P0 +5m mean **< 100 bps** (the cost-model charge on that tier). That tier cannot pay for itself on this instrument.
9. **FAST_LANE unused:** names that qualified *only* via fast (not classic RTH) have P0 +5m ≤ P5.

The 40-day read does not happen if calibration C1–C3 failed and were not reset (below).

## Cost

No change to yesterday's ranked movers and no change to what trades. Extra disk: one sidecar census per day plus about **20 reject 1-minute tapes** (capped below). The clock cost is the real one: the negative class exists only from the session this ships.

## 1. What gets recorded

Day files stay `{date, symbols:{SYM:[bars]}}` and contain **admitted movers only**. Mixing reject tapes into `state/days/` would change every pod's backtest. `loadRecordedDays` currently ingests every `*.json` in that folder (`lib/data.js:292-293`), so a sidecar named `YYYY-MM-DD.screen.json` in the same folder would be parsed as a day and break the library.

**Sidecar path: `state/screen/<YYYY-MM-DD>.json`.** Not under `state/days/`. A test must assert `loadRecordedDays` ignores it.

### 1a. In-memory census, updated every 90s poll, flushed at EOD

Snapshots and daily bars are already fetched for the whole RH-tradable universe. Persist the **failing values**, not a second fetch. Per symbol-day, keep first-touch and max-pct (and, after 16:00, max AH-pct vs today's close):

```
symbol
prevClose              // the ref discover() actually used (split-adjusted if the guard fired)
sessionAtFirst         // pm | rth | ah
firstSeenMin           // ET minute of the first poll that saw a print today
priceAtFirst, pctAtFirst, volAtFirst
maxPct, priceAtMaxPct, volAtMaxPct, minAtMaxPct
maxAhPct, ...          // vs today's official close; separate from RTH pct
failOrAdmit            // admit | price_lo | price_hi | pm_pct | pm_vol | rth_pct | rth_vol
                       // | fast_miss | ah_pct | ah_vol | rank | none
reasonAdmit            // pm | rth_classic | rth_fast | ah   (admit only)
causalRankAtFirstPoll  // rank among names that passed gates at that poll, 1-based
bestCausalRank         // best (lowest) causal rank during the day
eodRank                // DIAGNOSTIC ONLY — needs the full day
sampledTape            // bool
stratum                // see §2, or null if census-only
```

`prevClose` and first-poll minute are **not** "nice to have": without them P0 is undefined going forward, and reconstructing `prevClose` from a later daily bar is Fizz's HYP-007 falsifier #3 (the daily bar itself moves during RTH). Record what `discover()` used.

Volume: PM/AH use session cumulative from the 1-min bars of **finalists** today; RTH uses the daily-bar volume `discover()` already has. For census rejects we will often have only the snapshot/daily-bar volume. Store that, and tag `volSource: snapshot|daily|tape`. Do not invent a 1-min cumulative for a name we did not fetch.

### 1b. First-poll vs first-cross

| field | what it is | a policy using it needs to know |
|---|---|---|
| `firstSeenPollMin` | first 90s poll at which the name was in the **returned** top-`MAX_UNIVERSE` list | what the live engine knew |
| `firstCrossMin` | first 1-min bar the live gates would have been true | the tape between polls — up to 90s of hindsight vs live |

**P0 uses `firstSeenPollMin`.** `firstCrossMin` is a diagnostic of polling delay (D3), not a benchmark. Backfill already replays every minute and is optimistic on cadence; do not bake that into the tradable policy.

### 1c. Admitted tapes

Keep writing them to `state/days/` as today. Also write the census row. Do not wait for the sidecar to exist before recording days — a day with tapes and no census is a broken instrument day and does not count toward N=40.

### 1d. Reject tapes

1-minute 04:00–20:00 ET bars, same shape as day-file bars, stored **inside the sidecar** under `tapes:{SYM:[bars]}` (or `state/screen-tape/<date>/<SYM>.json` if the sidecar would exceed a reasonable write). They must not appear in `day.symbols`.

Fetch reject tapes **once, in the existing EOD record pass** (`engine.js:429-440`), not every 90s.

### 1e. Conscious omissions

- RH-universe rejects (OTC, warrants) — not a screen-threshold question.
- Names with no print today — not a negative class, an empty tape.
- Historical reconstruction of rejects — impossible; do not pretend Alpaca "could have" for 144 days.
- Live threshold changes — collection only.

HYP-007's four guesses are all in. Added: causal vs EOD rank, poll vs cross, vol source, stratum, sham class, sidecar isolation, census-vs-tape split.

## 2. Sampling the negative class

**Census everyone. Tape a stratified near-miss plus a sham. Never tape "a random failed name."** Far rejects (pct ≈ 0 with no volume) are a different market; a sample of them will always make the screen look good.

If a stratum is short that day, **leave it short**. Do not top up from another stratum. A biased sample is worse than a small one.

### Caps per session (tape)

| stratum | n | definition (all: had a print, not later admitted) |
|---|---|---|
| near_pct_pm | 4 | PM, 5 ≤ maxPct < `PM_PCT_FLOOR` (10) |
| near_pct_rth | 6 | RTH, 15 ≤ maxPct < `RTH_PCT_FLOOR` (25), daily vol ≥ `FAST_VOL_FLOOR` |
| near_pct_ah | 2 | AH, 5 ≤ maxAhPct < 10 |
| near_vol_pm | 2 | PM pct ≥ 10, session vol < `PM_MIN_VOL` |
| near_vol_rth | 2 | RTH pct ≥ 25, `FAST_VOL_FLOOR` ≤ day vol < `MIN_DAY_VOL` |
| rank_41_60 | 4 | passed the live gates, causal rank 41–60 at some poll (21–40 are already in `noteMovers` — tag them, do not double-fetch) |
| price_hi | 1 | price > `MAX_PRICE`, pct would pass the session floor |
| sham | 6 | 0 ≤ maxPct < 2, price in [`MIN_PRICE`, `MAX_PRICE`], not in another stratum |

**~21 extra tapes/day.** Ranks 21–40 already get day-file tapes via `moversSeenToday`; they are N2 (overflow), not a new fetch.

### Draw, deterministic

Within a stratum: sort by **distance to the relevant threshold** (closest first), tie-break by symbol. Take n. No "last poll wins", no arrival order (that is a time-of-day bias and a batch-offset bias).

A name that fails at 09:35 and later admits is **admitted**. It leaves the reject sample. Its first-poll minute is the admit poll.

### N1 definition (the pooled near-miss used in falsifier #2)

Union of `near_pct_pm`, `near_pct_rth`, `near_pct_ah` **tapes**. Volume-miss and rank-overflow and sham are **not** in N1. Mixing "didn't go up enough" with "went up on no volume" and "went up and we ranked it 45th" is how an empty screen hides.

## 3. Policies

Every policy states what it has to know. If it needs the future, it is a diagnostic.

Fill: next bar's open after the decision minute, wall-clock +5/15/30/60m, drop if nothing printed, report drop rates per arm. First unblinding is **cost-free forward returns** (same reason as `edge.js`: the cost model is a different HYP). A costed backtest is required before any live floor change, and is not this experiment.

### Executable (benchmarks)

| id | rule | knows | does not know |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | enter at `firstSeenPollMin` on admitted names | the name just appeared in the live top-20 | future bars, which 15 minutes will burst, EOD rank |
| **P1** | enter at the pod `signalAt` that would have fired (current system) | screen admission + the trigger | — |
| **P2** | enter at first-poll + 1 / 5 / 15 / 30 minutes | T_poll and a clock | the trigger, the future path |
| **P3a/b/c** | P0 restricted to causal rank at first poll ≤ 10 / 20 / 40 | causal rank so far today | EOD rank |
| **P3d** | P0 on causal ranks 21–40 only | ditto | — |
| **P4** | looser/tighter floors on near-miss tapes: first poll the looser gate is true. Bands: PM 5, RTH 15, RTH 35, no-fast-lane | the looser gate at a live poll | that the live screen later did or did not take them |
| **P5** | enter at first-seen poll on the **sham** class | a name printed and was quiet | anything about movers |
| **P6** | P0 split at price-at-first-poll < $2 vs ≥ $2 | price | — |

P2 is a **curve**, not one RNG draw. Report all four delays, paired against P0 on the same names (same-name difference, median, drop-top-5%, unique symbol-days). Red Team's addendum on `ae685af`: unpaired RNG after-controls are how a ratio gets invented.

P0 vs P1 is the trigger-vs-screen decomposition. It is **not** the screen-vs-nothing test. P0 ≈ P1 is consistent with a useless trigger; P0 vs P5 / N1 is the screen.

### Diagnostics (not benchmarks — labelled)

| id | rule | why it is not tradable |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | EOD rank cutoffs | needs the close |
| D2 | enter the best 15-minute block after admission | needs the path |
| D3 | enter at `firstCrossMin` from 1-min tape | knows the crossing between 90s polls |

## 4. Calibration — assume the instrument is broken

Three checks that must return a **known** value. If they fail, do not read P0 vs P5. Fix the recorder, **reset the 40-day clock**.

**C1 — sham has no urgency.** On sham names, mean(+5m at first-seen) − mean(+5m at first-seen+5m) is within **±15 bps**. Quiet names must not reward "being early." If they do, the instrument is measuring open-drift or print density, not the screen. (The analogue of Builder's flag-at-0.0% dead draws.)

**C2 — empty gate is empty.** A fake admit rule `pct >= 1000` must print **n=0**, not a mean of 0.0 on an empty array. Silent zero-from-empty is how a broken join looks like a null result.

**C3 — drop rates are reported per arm**, P0 / P5 / N1 / each P2 delay, signal and control. An arm that drops >10% more samples than its pair is not comparable; the read is invalid until the drop rule is the same.

**C1–C3 may be inspected at day 10. Nothing else may.** Looking at P0 vs P5 at day 10 spends the experiment. If C1–C3 fail at day 10, fix and restart; those 10 days do not count toward 40.

## 5. How many days before it can be read

**N_CALIBRATE = 10** full sessions with census + tape sample. C1–C3 only.

**N_READ = 40** full sessions after a passing calibration (or after a reset). First unblinding of P0 / P5 / N1 / P2 / P3 / P4 / P6 / falsifiers 1–9.

A session counts only if (a) the sidecar exists, (b) at least one admitted census row has `firstSeenPollMin` and `prevClose`, (c) the sham stratum has ≥1 tape **or** was honestly empty (logged, not backfilled). A day that recorded tapes and no census does not count.

**Do not peek.** Running totals of P0 vs P5 before day 40 are contamination of the same kind that killed HYP-001. If someone looks, the clock restarts.

40 is the number because this experiment is at the **symbol-day** grain (~20 admitted first-polls/day → ~800 events; near-miss ~12 tapes/day → ~480). Session slices (PM/RTH/AH) are then ~hundreds, which is enough to see a 50 bps gap and not enough to mine one. 20 days would make the session cuts too thin; 144 is a holdout we will not have this quarter.

A second 40-day confirm after changing a live floor is a **new** HYP, not a free look at the same tape.

## What this can conclude, and what it cannot

It can conclude the live screen selects names whose next 5 and 30 minutes differ from sham and from near-miss, or that it does not. It can conclude a named threshold is arbitrary or too tight/loose on that evidence.

It cannot conclude a trigger is good (P1 is descriptive). It cannot conclude a change is profitable after costs, stops, the participation cap, or flatten (HYP-005). It cannot rescue the 144-day library.

If P0 fails falsifiers 1 and 2, **the screen has no measured value.** That is an allowed and useful outcome. Do not redesign the sampler after seeing it.
