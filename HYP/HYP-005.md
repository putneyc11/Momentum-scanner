# HYP-005 — the participation cap is entry-only, so every exit in the repo is unconstrained

**Status:** proposed
**Date:** 2026-08-28  **Pod:** (all — this is the backtester, not a pod)

## Claim

`MAX_BAR_PARTICIPATION` is applied when sizing an entry (`lib/backtest.js:105`)
and **nowhere else**. `closeOut` (`lib/backtest.js:39`) sells the whole position
at the bar's close with no volume constraint. Measured on surge's first-expansion
entry (HYP-003), across its 179 flatten/eod exits:

```
exit size as a share of that ENTIRE minute's volume
  median 48.0%   p75 185.6%   p90 620.1%   p95 786.9%   max 2322%

  demanding >10%  of the bar:  102 of 125  (81.6%)
  demanding >50%  of the bar:   61         (48.8%)
  demanding >100% of the bar:   48         (38.4%)
```

**38.4% of those exits sell more shares than traded in that minute.** A further
54 of the 179 exit at a minute with **no bar at all** — `eod` closes at
`bars[length-1].c` stamped with a synthetic minute 1200.

This is the fill-fantasy bug the repo already fixed once, on the side the fix
never reached.

## Category

`lib/backtest.js` execution model. Not a pod, not a parameter.

## Mechanism

Entries were capped because an oversized entry invents its own fill price. An
oversized *exit* does exactly the same thing in reverse, and it matters more
here because the exits that carry the profit are concentrated at 19:55 on
after-hours tape, which is the thinnest and widest-spread part of the session.

## Why it is not a caveat

HYP-003's entire improvement (0.53 → 0.92) is realised in flatten and eod
exits — +142,144 and +18,731 — and **100% of those exit after 16:00 ET**.
The result is also extremely concentrated:

```
PF with the top N winners removed
  drop top   0:  0.918        drop top  25:  0.544
  drop top   5:  0.777        drop top 100:  0.201
```

Five trades out of 1,728 carry roughly a fifth of the profit factor, and they
are in the bucket that could not have been filled.

**moon is affected the same way** — 153 flatten/eod exits, median 33.6% of the
bar, 28.6% demanding over 100%. redgreen and reclaim barely use flatten exits
and are largely unaffected.

## Falsifier

Written before the fix exists. This is wrong if:

1. Capping exits at `MAX_BAR_PARTICIPATION` with the remainder carried to the
   next bar leaves surge's PF materially unchanged (within 0.03). That would
   mean the oversized exits were not where the money was.
2. The affected exits turn out to be concentrated in liquid names where the
   modelled size is genuinely absorbable, and the volume figures here are an
   artefact of the day-file build rather than the tape.
3. Live fill data shows the engine routinely gets full size at the close price
   in after-hours on these names.

## Cost

No discovery change. Expect **every profit factor in the repo to fall**,
worst on moon and surge, negligibly on redgreen and reclaim.

## Two decisions this needs, which are not mine to make alone

1. **The 19:55 flatten cannot partially fill and then stop** — no overnight
   exposure is the whole point of it. Either it pays the slippage of a forced
   exit into thin tape, or the position genuinely carries overnight and the
   backtest must model that. Today it pretends neither happens.
2. **Live does not get the modelled close.** It sells through a real book.
   So this divergence has been making backtest optimistic against live all
   along, and closing it changes what "backtest PF" has meant historically.

## Result  (builder)

Implemented on `hyp/005` off `a380bc5`. `node engine.js test`: **161 passed,
0 failed** (146 before; fifteen new assertions, one per gate of the new model).

### What was built

Exits are capped at `MAX_BAR_PARTICIPATION` of the exit bar exactly as entries
are. What does not fit **stays open and goes back through `exitCheck` next
bar**, so a carried remainder is a live position: it can stop out, ratchet, hit
a target, or leave for a different reason than the one that started the unwind.
It keeps its slot against `maxPositions` until flat. The only exception is the
end-of-day residue, which cannot carry because there is no overnight — it is
forced at any size, pays slippage scaled by how many times the cap it demands,
clamped by the new frozen `MAX_FORCED_SLIP_BPS`, and every such fill is tagged.

### The fiction is gone

surge (first-expansion entry), exits as a share of their own bar's volume:

```
before   median 57.6%   >10% 83.8%   >100% 40.8%   max 2634%
after    median 10.0%   >10%  0.0%   >100%  0.0%   max   10%
```

### Regression gate — FAILS, by design

```
$ node engine.js regress --base a380bc5

regression gate: working tree vs a380bc5 over 144 recorded days
default params, fail if any pod loses more than 0.03 profit factor

pod           base PF   head PF     delta   base trades   head trades   status
moon             0.88      0.81     -0.07          1802          3084   REGRESSED
surge            0.53      0.49     -0.04          1638          2243   REGRESSED
flag              0.5      0.47     -0.03          3379          4410   ok
redgreen         0.64      0.64      0.00          2859          4503   ok
igniter          0.49       0.5     +0.01          2708          3826   ok
gapgo            0.61      0.64     +0.03          4613          6812   ok
reclaim          0.44      0.48     +0.04          3975          7015   ok

FAIL — at least one pod lost edge. Do not merge without explaining why that is acceptable.
```

**Read the netPct column, not the profit factor.** Splitting one exit into
several fills changes the number of trade *records*, and profit factor is
computed over records, so it moves mechanically whether or not the economics
move. Measured directly:

| pod | positions before → after | netPct before → after | PF before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| reclaim | 3,856 → 4,004 | −99.12 → **−99.11** | 0.44 → 0.48 |
| gapgo | 4,482 → 4,513 | −98.78 → **−98.46** | 0.61 → 0.64 |
| redgreen | 2,733 → 2,589 | −85.76 → −85.21 | 0.64 → 0.64 |
| moon | 1,802 → 1,635 | −44.45 → **−56.08** | 0.88 → 0.81 |

reclaim and gapgo did **not** improve. Their net P&L is unchanged to two
decimal places while their profit factor rose — that is granularity, not edge.
**moon is the only genuine regression**, and it is larger than its PF suggests:
−44.45 → −56.08 net.

This matters beyond this change: **the gate's ±0.03 profit-factor threshold is
not invariant to fill granularity**, so any future change that splits or merges
fills will move every pod's PF for free. Worth its own follow-up.

### HYP-003 does not collapse

The expectation was that surge's 0.92 would fall a long way toward the 0.53 it
replaced. Compared like with like, under the same capped model:

| surge entry | uncapped | **capped** |
|---|---|---|
| 3-bar climax (old) | 0.53 / −95.11 | **0.49 / −96.14** |
| first expansion (HYP-003) | 0.92 / −27.04 | **0.75 / −68.61** |

The entry change is worth **+0.26 profit factor under the honest model**, down
from +0.39 under the fictional one. On netPct — the granularity-invariant
measure — the gain shrinks from +68.1 points to +27.5, so roughly 60% of it was
fill fantasy and 40% survives. **HYP-003 is materially weakened and not
refuted.** It is still a losing pod at 0.75.

### The two things the ruling required be reported

surge (first-expansion) under the cap:

**Carried remainders cut both ways, and net they HURT.**
```
1,442 fills (47.5%) are the remainder of an earlier attempt
  better price than the first attempt:  278 (19.3%)
  worse  price than the first attempt:  311 (21.6%)
  net effect of carrying:            −33,616
  bars waited — median 4, max 799
```
So the two-sidedness is real and it is not quietly propping the result up:
letting remainders re-evaluate costs money on net, which is the direction that
makes the model more pessimistic rather than less.

**The forced residue is negligible and is not load-bearing.**
```
122 forced fills (4.0% of all fills), PnL −3,008 of −68,610
profit factor WITHOUT the forced residue: 0.719   (with: 0.75)
```
Removing the one remaining fiction makes the result slightly *worse*, so nothing
here rests on it. That is the number to check first on any future result.
